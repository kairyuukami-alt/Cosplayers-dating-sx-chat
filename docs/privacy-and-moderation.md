# Privacy and moderation model

Cosplay Match keeps private dating conversations visible only to the matched participants. Platform administrators do not receive a hidden universal inbox for private chats.

The creator/admin account can contact any registered user through a separate Platform Support conversation. Users can always distinguish support messages from dating messages.

Users may delete messages they sent. Media messages can optionally be sent as view-once. View-once reduces normal re-opening but cannot prevent screenshots, screen recording, camera capture, or a modified client.

The application does not silently duplicate chat photos or videos to Google Drive or another undisclosed backup. Any future backup or retention mechanism should be disclosed in product copy and the privacy policy before collecting user content.
